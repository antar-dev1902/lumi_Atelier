# Baby’s First Website — Track 2

## Character sheet

Name: Lumi.
What she is: An original fluffy moth with big amber eyes, mismatched antennae and stitched pastel wings. Curious, kind and resourceful, with an exacting eye for fabric.
What she does: Independent fashion designer exploring eveningwear, architectural tailoring and playful silhouettes.
Her story: Grandmother’s attic taught her to see possibility in forgotten fabric. Sketches became a first collection, years of studio practice sharpened her craft, and a last-minute runway repair gave her the confidence to open her own atelier.
Tagline: Every thread deserves another story.

## Build description

Lumi’s Atelier imagines a tiny fashion designer who gives forgotten threads another life. We connected her moth identity with textile wings, linen books, swatches and a grandmother’s sewing room. Visitors can experience her origin or enter her collections immediately.

The central story moment is Blue Hour: a torn runway bodice is draped, pinned, stitched and revealed under the spotlight, then becomes the first page of a real interactive lookbook. Scroll, explicit stage buttons and reduced-motion stills provide different ways to experience it. The three collections contain twelve looks with original vector studies, editable sketch colours and a saved pinboard.

The portfolio also remembers returning visitors, offers authored dialogue, hides a first-design discovery and includes a shared guestbook and deduplicated visitor counter. The worktable changes composition across screens; its Flutter control moves the books and gives Lumi a new place to land. Sound and narration have separate visible controls, and the mobile catalogue keeps the outfit and page controls together.

The entry uses React and Vite with a small Node server. AI-generated original character and studio art are allowed by the brief and are credited visibly. Real fashion photographs illustrate fictional collection narratives; each photographer and source is credited.
